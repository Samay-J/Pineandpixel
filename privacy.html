Honest Pin privacy policy

Pixel & Pine · effective 15 November 2026

Honest Pin is a puzzle game made by Pixel & Pine ("we"). This page explains what information the game and the advertising inside it use, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any account. The game has no sign-in. Your progress, coins, plants and settings are saved only on your own device and never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. You may see a full-screen ad between levels (never during a level, and not in the first levels), and you can choose to watch an ad to double the coins for a level or to get a hint. To show and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
  • your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location,
  • information about the device and the app (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps), and diagnostic data about ad performance.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, personalise them where you allow it, measure them, and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch you are asked for consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & ads.
  • Everyone on Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Watching rewarded ads is always optional.

Children

Honest Pin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are limited to content suitable for general and parental-guidance audiences.

Security and retention

Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit. We keep no data about you on our own servers because we do not run any. Google's retention is described in its privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
